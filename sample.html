What makes Thekua so special?

Thekua (also called Thekwa or Khajuria) is a beloved traditional sweet made during Chhath Puja, weddings and festivals. At Tasty Thekua, every piece is shaped by hand using wooden moulds — just as it was made in Bihar and eastern UP for centuries.

Whole wheat & desi jaggery
We use stone-ground atta and unrefined gur sourced from local farmers — the natural sweetness you can taste.
Deep-fried in pure cow ghee
No dalda, no refined oil. The ghee gives each piece its signature richness and extends freshness naturally.
Made fresh, packed fresh
Sealed in food-grade airtight containers the same day. Stays fresh for 2 weeks at room temperature.

What exactly is Thekua made from?
Thekua is made from stone-ground whole wheat flour (atta), unrefined desi jaggery (gur), pure cow ghee, cardamom and fennel seeds. Some variants include fresh coconut, sesame, or dry fruits. We use absolutely no dalda, refined oil, artificial colours or preservatives.
Do you deliver free in Howrah too?
Yes — completely free delivery across all of Howrah including Shibpur, Liluah, Bally, Santragachi, Salkia, Golabari and surrounding areas. No delivery charge for any order size.
How long does Thekua stay fresh?
In an airtight container at room temperature — 2 weeks. Refrigerated — up to 1 month. The pure ghee acts as a natural preservative. We pack in food-grade airtight containers to ensure this.
Can I order the same day?
Yes — same-day delivery is available for orders placed before 2 PM. For larger orders (2kg+) we may need 24 hours' notice. WhatsApp us and we'll confirm timing right away.
Do I need to pay online?
No online payment whatsoever. We accept cash on delivery only — you pay when the Thekua arrives at your door. Zero prepayment risk.
How do I pre-book for Chhath Puja?
Chhath demand is very high. We recommend pre-booking at least 3–5 days in advance. Simply WhatsApp us with your name, quantity and delivery address in Kolkata or Howrah and we'll confirm your slot.
