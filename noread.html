Thekua is a traditional Indian sweet biscuit made from whole wheat flour, jaggery (gur), coconut and spices like cardamom and fennel seeds. It has a crispy texture with a rich, natural sweetness — earthy from jaggery, aromatic from spices. It is especially popular during Chhath Puja as prasad but enjoyed year-round as a wholesome snack.
